## Adopted

- Independent reviewers seek disconfirming evidence, state what evidence would
  change a material conclusion, and do not treat consensus or confidence as proof.
- Flaky reruns remain failures until the root cause is fixed or the test is
  visibly quarantined with ownership.
- Backend integrations use finite timeouts and only bounded, backoff-and-jitter
  retries that are safe from duplicate effects.
- Security review validates AI tool choice and parameters outside model prose and
  re-authorizes consequential actions at execution.
- Host-bridge promotion requires effective-sandbox attestation, action/input
  hashes, fresh receipt-bound verifier context, and no recursive delegation.
- Current lifecycle documentation now states its real single-attempt behavior.
- The kernel derives an immutable proportional validation lane from trusted risk,
  actions, gates, scope, and changed paths; callers cannot downgrade it.
- Project inspection distinguishes exact package-lock evidence from declaration
  ranges and unresolved versions. Decision-driven research is finite,
  version-matched, and non-browsing by default.
- High-risk work has an explicit adversarial-review gate, while stateful API,
  migration, integration, and AI-system scenarios require applicable partial
  failure, duplicate/retry, timeout/cancellation, concurrency, stale-state, and
  recovery evidence.
- Tool-call receipts fail closed unless each call binds once to an eligible,
  selected, inspected project script and its authorized action/input digest.
  Spawn/delegation calls are forbidden, and independent verifier receipts carry
  disconfirming checks and reversal criteria.
- UI review now covers applicable permission, offline, partial, stale,
  confirmation, cancellation, and recovery states plus project-declared locale
  and directionality behavior without imposing a universal regional policy.

These controls enhance existing agents. They do not add a researcher, red-team,
read-only duplicate, writer duplicate, or looping agent. Research and adversarial
review remain bounded task modes owned by the existing architect, domain,
reviewer, skill-scout, QA, and security roles as applicable.

## Rejected

- The document's claim to be a single canonical instruction source for Visual
  Twin / RISS is project-specific and cannot replace toolkit registries,
  scoped instructions, generators, or runtime contracts.
- Saudi legal, regulatory, localization, and product assumptions are not added
  to the universal toolkit core. They require a separately governed project or
  regional overlay and accountable legal/policy review.
- A fixed two-correction restart rule is not adopted as universal runtime policy.
  Current v0.3 is single-attempt; future retries require an explicit, tested
  convergence contract.
- Named standards and revision claims are not treated as certification or a
  compliance claim. Their applicability, revision, and review receipts remain
  governed separately.

## Deferred

- Dynamic read-only versus scoped-write invocation for one logical domain agent
  remains deferred until a host bridge can attest the effective sandbox for each
  assignment. Prompt wording alone cannot enforce a permission downgrade.
- Per-assignment context refresh and receipt-bound writer-to-verifier handoff
  remain host-bridge promotion requirements until implemented and adversarially
  tested.
- Attempt-sequence, retry, and flaky-result receipts remain deferred to the host
  bridge. Until a closed attempt schema exists, one selected executable tool may
  be observed only once per assignment receipt; a rerun cannot be laundered into
  a pass.
- Local database migration-file authorship remains intentionally unowned by the
  generic writer until a separate contract proves scoped paths, destructive and
  backfill controls, independent database review, and a non-live validation path.
- A low-risk direct execution lane may reduce orchestration cost, but it requires
  measured task/pilot evidence and must preserve high-risk independent review.

## Disposition

Selective clean-room enhancement only. The source remains advisory and is not
activated, imported, installed, or represented as an approved standard.
